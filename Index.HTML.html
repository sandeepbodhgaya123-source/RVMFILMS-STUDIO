Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How can I confirm my wedding shoot date with RVM Films Studio?

You can check date availability directly on this booking form by selecting your start and end dates. To lock the date, fill in your details, select your packages, pay the advance amount via UPI/Cash, and click "Confirm & Lock Date".

2. Where is the studio located?

Our studio is located at Domuhan, Bodhgaya, Gaya (Bihar). You can contact proprietor Sandeep Kumar directly at +91 9852044548 or 7033315162.

3. How long is my wedding data stored securely?

Your raw footage and edited data are safely stored in our studio archives for 2 to 3 months following your event. We recommend clearing your remaining balance and collecting your hard drives/pen drives within this timeframe.

4. Can I track my booking status online?

Yes! Click on the "Track Booking Status" button at the top of the page, enter your mobile number or invoice ID, and view real-time milestones, assigned crew, and payment ledgers.
